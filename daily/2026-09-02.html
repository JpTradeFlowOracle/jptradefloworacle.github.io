What is EDINET and why is this data important?

EDINET (Electronic Disclosure for Investors NETwork) is the official disclosure platform operated by Japan's Financial Services Agency. It contains mandatory filings including tender offers, large shareholder reports (5%+ ownership changes), extraordinary reports, and share buyback disclosures. JP Disclosure Intelligence structures and translates this data into English daily.

M&A / Restructuring Nippon Seiki Co., Ltd.

Nippon Seiki divests European software unit to Vietnamese ICT leader's new Polish entity.

Nippon Seiki's European subsidiary is transferring its in-vehicle embedded software development business in Poland to FPT Poland, a newly formed entity of Vietnam's FPT Group.   • Nippon Seiki Europe is divesting its in-vehicle embedded software development business.
  • The transferee is FPT Poland Sp. z o.o., established by FPT Group on July 22, 2026.
  • Approximately 50 employees are part of the transferred business.
  • The contract was signed on September 1, 2026, with an effective date of October 2, 2026.
  • Nippon Seiki plans to outsource software development to FPT Poland post-transfer.

Tender Offer (TOB) Itochu Corporation

Earnings Report Uchida Yoko Co., Ltd.

Record profits driven by strong public and private sector demand, despite cautious outlook.

Uchida Yoko reported record-high revenues and profits for the fiscal year ending July 2026, significantly exceeding prior year results. This strong performance was fueled by robust demand in both public sector ICT, particularly GIGA School terminal renewals, and private sector office environment solutions. The cautious but still strong outlook suggests a solid underlying business despite the expected normalization of some demand.

  • The company increased its year-end dividend to 76.00 JPY per share, up from the previously announced 72.00 JPY.

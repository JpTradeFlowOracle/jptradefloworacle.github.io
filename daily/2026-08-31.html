What is EDINET and why is this data important?

EDINET (Electronic Disclosure for Investors NETwork) is the official disclosure platform operated by Japan's Financial Services Agency. It contains mandatory filings including tender offers, large shareholder reports (5%+ ownership changes), extraordinary reports, and share buyback disclosures. JP Disclosure Intelligence structures and translates this data into English daily.

M&A / Restructuring G-Monster Lab

  • The stock exchange became effective on August 31, 2026.
  • Monster Lab Omnibus (MLO) is no longer a consolidated subsidiary.

  • SAMI Japan is now an equity-method affiliate of G-Monster Lab.
  • G-Monster Lab currently holds 334,334 shares in SAMI Japan, representing 34.91% of voting rights.
  • Following a planned additional share transfer, G-Monster Lab's voting rights in SAMI Japan will be 30.39%.

Tender Offer (TOB) Itochu Corporation

Itochu to launch JPY 2,880/share tender offer for Dentsu Soken's privatization.

Itochu, through a joint venture, plans a tender offer at JPY 2,880 per share to privatize Dentsu Soken. This strategic move aims to integrate Itochu's IT capabilities with Dentsu Soken's consulting strengths, fostering agile decision-making and accelerating growth away from public market pressures. The Dentsu Soken board supports the offer, recommending shareholders tender their shares.

This privatization highlights a trend of Japanese conglomerates consolidating digital assets for deeper integration and value creation, leveraging synergies between a trading house and an IT services firm.

  • Tender offer price: JPY 2,880 per share.
  • Minimum shares to be acquired: 9,340,400 (4.78% of voting rights).
  • Expected launch date: Early November 2026, pending regulatory approvals.
  • Current majority shareholder (Dentsu Group, 61.78%) will retain its stake, not tendering shares.
  • Dentsu Soken's board recommends shareholders accept the offer.
  • Dentsu Soken will cancel its December 2026 year-end dividend upon successful privatization.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should consider the JPY 2,880/share offer, which includes a significant premium over pre-speculation prices, and the board's recommendation to tender shares for privatization.

Tender Offer (TOB) TWINBIRD CORPORATION

Twinbird rejects Japanet's tender offer, prioritizing independent growth strategy.

Twinbird's board has unanimously rejected Japanet Holdings' proposed tender offer for full acquisition, citing significant dis-synergies and a lack of concrete evidence for Japanet's claimed benefits. The company believes the acquisition would jeopardize its existing retail relationships, dilute its high-value brand, and disrupt its specialized B2B and product development focus. Twinbird asserts its newly announced mid-term management plan offers superior long-term value for shareholders through independent operation.

This rejection signals a firm stance against a potential hostile takeover, highlighting Twinbird's commitment to its niche market and independent strategic direction. It challenges Japanet to either significantly revise its offer or reconsider its acquisition strategy, while also presenting investors with a clear alternative valuation based on Twinbird's standalone growth plan.

  • Twinbird's board unanimously rejected Japanet's tender offer.
  • The company estimates a potential loss of approximately JPY 2.3 billion in annual sales from existing retail channels due to dis-synergies.
  • Twinbird's new mid-term plan targets JPY 11.7 billion in revenue and JPY 1.4 billion in operating profit by February 2031.
  • The mid-term plan aims for an ROE of 13% or more and a total shareholder return ratio of 75-100% over the plan period.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should weigh Twinbird's articulated dis-synergies and its ambitious new mid-term plan's value against the possibility of a revised, higher offer from Japanet, considering the implications for both independent and acquired scenarios.

Tender Offer (TOB) Twinbird Corporation

Twinbird opposes Japanet's 800 JPY per share tender offer, citing significant dis-synergies.

Twinbird's board, based on its special committee's recommendation, has decided to oppose Japanet Holdings' planned tender offer of 800 JPY per share. The company believes the proposed acquisition would lead to substantial negative impacts, including customer attrition and disruption to its B2B strategy and supply chain, outweighing any potential synergies. Twinbird asserts its own new mid-term plan offers a more robust path to enhanced corporate value and shareholder returns.

This rejection signals a firm commitment to Twinbird's independent growth strategy, potentially deterring future unsolicited bids at this valuation and highlighting the importance of non-financial considerations in M&A.

  • Japanet Holdings proposed a tender offer at 800 JPY per share.
  • Twinbird's special committee identified risks including potential loss of JPY 2.3 billion in sales from major retailers in the first year post-acquisition.
  • The company's new mid-term plan aims to increase gross profit margin from 23.7% to 36.3% by FY2031/2.
  • Twinbird plans to allocate approximately 30% of its JPY 60 billion cash generation over five years to shareholder returns, targeting a 75-100% total return ratio.
  • The tender offer was expected to commence in late October 2026 for 30 business days.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should note Twinbird's strong conviction in its standalone growth plan and its willingness to reject an offer deemed detrimental to long-term value, despite the premium.

Tender Offer (TOB) SBINM G.K.

SBINM launches tender offer for BASE to establish equity-method affiliation and strategic alliance.

SBINM G.K., a subsidiary of SBI Holdings, has initiated a tender offer to acquire up to 20.00% of BASE Corporation's shares at JPY 340 per share. The aim is to form a capital and business alliance, making BASE an equity-method affiliate while maintaining its public listing and independence on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth market.

This transaction signifies a strategic move by SBI Group to integrate its financial, media, and IP assets with BASE's e-commerce and payment platforms, aiming to create new synergies and expand customer value in the digital economy without delisting the target.

  • Offer price is JPY 340 per common share.
  • Tender offer period is from August 31, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
  • Acquirer seeks to obtain up to 23,792,300 shares, representing 20.00% of BASE's voting rights.
  • The offer price represents a 9.32% premium over BASE's closing price on August 27, 2026.
  • BASE's board, including all independent outside directors, supports the offer and recommends shareholders consider it.
  • Funding for the acquisition, totaling JPY 9 billion, is secured through a loan from SBI Holdings.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should note the premium offered and the strategic rationale for the alliance, while recognizing that BASE will remain listed and the offer has no minimum acceptance threshold, potentially leading to pro-rata purchases if oversubscribed.

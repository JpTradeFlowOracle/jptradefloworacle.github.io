What is EDINET and why is this data important?

EDINET (Electronic Disclosure for Investors NETwork) is the official disclosure platform operated by Japan's Financial Services Agency. It contains mandatory filings including tender offers, large shareholder reports (5%+ ownership changes), extraordinary reports, and share buyback disclosures. JP Disclosure Intelligence structures and translates this data into English daily.

M&A / Restructuring YAC Holdings Co., Ltd.

YAC Holdings consolidates three subsidiaries to integrate technologies and boost growth.

YAC Holdings is streamlining its group structure by merging three wholly-owned subsidiaries, YAC Mechatronics, YAC Machinery, and YAC Dustech, into a single entity. This strategic consolidation aims to integrate specialized mechatronics technologies, enhance operational efficiency, and accelerate resource allocation towards growth sectors, aligning with the group's ambitious 2030 sales target.

This internal restructuring signifies a proactive effort to optimize the group's capabilities in automation, precision conveyance, and cutting technologies. By fostering greater synergy and efficiency, the company aims to strengthen its market position and accelerate innovation in high-value-added product and service offerings.

  • YAC Mechatronics will be the surviving entity, absorbing YAC Machinery and YAC Dustech.
  • The merger is scheduled to become effective on October 1, 2026.
  • The initiative supports the group's strategic objective of reaching JPY 100 billion in sales by 2030.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should recognize this internal consolidation as a strategic move to unlock synergies and accelerate long-term growth, with the company projecting a minor impact on immediate consolidated performance.

M&A / Restructuring Asax Co., Ltd.

Asax to absorb wholly-owned real estate subsidiary Foresight for operational streamlining.

Asax announced an absorption-type merger with its wholly-owned subsidiary, Foresight Co., Ltd., effective September 28, 2026. This internal restructuring aims to consolidate overlapping real estate leasing operations within the group, enhancing management efficiency and streamlining business processes.

This internal consolidation reflects Asax's strategic focus on optimizing its real estate segment by eliminating redundancies and improving operational synergy, signaling a drive for greater group-wide efficiency.

  • The merger is scheduled to become effective on September 28, 2026.
  • Foresight, the absorbed wholly-owned subsidiary, reported net assets of JPY 67.7 million and operating revenue of JPY 44.9 million for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026.
  • As a simple and short-form merger, no shareholder approval is required for either company.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should note this internal consolidation is expected to have a minor impact on Asax's consolidated financial performance, primarily targeting operational efficiencies.

Tender Offer (TOB) Baudolair Inc.

Baudolair to be taken private by Bain Capital-advised fund via tender offer.

Baudolair, a Prime Market listed company, is the target of a tender offer by a Bain Capital-advised fund, aiming for a management buyout and subsequent delisting. This transaction will transition the company to private ownership, facilitating strategic initiatives away from public market pressures. The company's management supports the offer and will remain involved post-privatization.

This privatization represents a significant strategic shift for Baudolair, enabling long-term value creation and operational restructuring without the constraints of public market reporting and short-term investor expectations. Its delisting will remove a technology services provider from the Prime Market.

  • The tender offer price for common shares is JPY 2,970 per share.
  • The tender offer period runs from August 19, 2026, to October 5, 2026 (31 business days).
  • The minimum acquisition threshold is 3,135,200 shares, representing 9.55% of outstanding shares.
  • The acquirer aims to acquire all shares and delist Baudolair from the Prime Market.
  • Existing stock options (new share subscription rights) are offered at JPY 1 per unit.
  • Key management shareholders are selling their shares to the acquirer at JPY 2,350 per share in a parallel transaction.

Investor TakeawayShareholders should evaluate the JPY 2,970 offer price against their investment thesis, considering the impending delisting and the management's support for the transaction.

Management Change YONDOSHI HOLDINGS INC.

YONDOSHI Holdings allocates treasury shares to executive stock benefit trust.

The company is disposing of 30,000 treasury shares to an executive stock benefit trust via a third-party allotment. This action continues an existing incentive scheme designed to align executive interests with long-term corporate value creation. The shares will be acquired by the trust using an additional cash contribution.

This move reinforces the company's commitment to performance-linked executive compensation, potentially enhancing governance and driving sustainable growth. The minimal dilution suggests a balanced approach to incentive alignment.

  • 30,000 common shares will be disposed of.
  • The disposition price is JPY 2,133 per share, totaling JPY 63,990,000.
  • The shares will be allotted to Japan Custody Bank (as trustee) on September 3, 2026.
  • The dilution effect is approximately 0.12% of outstanding shares.
  • An additional JPY 49,000,000 cash contribution is being made to the trust.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should view this as a standard mechanism to incentivize management, with a minor dilutive impact on existing shareholders.

Earnings Report SMT Japan High Dividend Stock Active ETF

SMT Japan High Dividend Stock Active ETF sees significant asset growth and initiates distributions.

The SMT Japan High Dividend Stock Active ETF experienced substantial growth in net assets and outstanding units during the latest period, fueled by significant capital inflows. This expansion allowed the fund to declare its inaugural distribution per unit, aligning with its investment objective despite a period-over-period decline in net income.

The robust asset accumulation and initiation of distributions underscore increasing investor demand for actively managed, high-dividend Japanese equity exposure, potentially signaling broader market confidence in this investment strategy.

  • Net assets surged to JPY 1,092 million by July 11, 2026, from JPY 423 million six months prior.
  • Outstanding units more than doubled, reaching 350,000 units from 160,000 units in the previous period.
  • The fund declared its first distribution of JPY 3 per unit for the period ending July 2026.
  • Net asset value per unit increased to JPY 3,121 from JPY 2,646 over the six-month period.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should note the fund's rapid growth and its ability to commence distributions, reflecting both strong market interest and the successful execution of its high-dividend strategy.

Earnings Report SMT ETF Japan Stock Selection Active

Fund sees strong per-unit performance and higher distributions despite net asset decline.

The fund experienced a notable decrease in total net assets and outstanding units, primarily due to significant redemptions during the period. However, its per-unit performance was robust, with a substantial increase in net asset value per unit and a higher distribution per unit, indicating effective active management and strong underlying portfolio returns.

This performance suggests the fund's active management strategy is delivering value on a per-unit basis, potentially attracting new capital or retaining existing investors despite recent outflows and a reduction in overall fund size.

  • Net assets decreased to 382 million JPY from 449 million JPY.
  • Outstanding units fell from 160,000 to 110,000.
  • Net asset value per unit rose significantly to 3,475 JPY from 2,808 JPY.
  • Distributions per unit increased to 13 JPY from 11 JPY.
  • The fund experienced 120 million JPY in redemptions and 20 million JPY in subscriptions.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should focus on the fund's strong per-unit performance and increased distributions, which highlight effective active management despite a reduction in overall fund size due to net redemptions.

Earnings Report SMT ETF Domestic REIT Select Active Investment

Fund reports significant net asset value decline and operating loss due to J-REIT market downturn.

The fund experienced a substantial decline in net asset value per unit and an operating loss for the period ending July 2026, primarily driven by negative valuation adjustments on its J-REIT holdings. This performance contrasts sharply with the previous period's gains, reflecting a challenging market environment for Japanese real estate investment trusts.

The fund's performance highlights recent volatility and downward pressure within the Japanese REIT market, impacting investor returns for specialized real estate funds. This could signal broader sentiment shifts or specific sector headwinds for J-REITs.

  • Net asset value per unit decreased to JPY 2,087 from JPY 2,318 in the prior period.
  • The fund recorded an operating loss of JPY 110 million, a significant reversal from the JPY 188 million operating profit in the previous period.
  • Valuation losses on investment securities totaled JPY 101.7 million for the period.
  • Distribution per unit was reduced to JPY 45 from JPY 53 previously.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should note the fund's negative performance driven by J-REIT market conditions and the resulting reduction in distributions, indicating potential continued headwinds for the sector.

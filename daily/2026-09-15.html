What is EDINET and why is this data important?

EDINET (Electronic Disclosure for Investors NETwork) is the official disclosure platform operated by Japan's Financial Services Agency. It contains mandatory filings including tender offers, large shareholder reports (5%+ ownership changes), extraordinary reports, and share buyback disclosures. JP Disclosure Intelligence structures and translates this data into English daily.

Tender Offer (TOB) JPMC Corporation

Tender offer for JPMC shares revised with higher price, extended period, and lower minimum.

The tender offer for JPMC common shares has been amended, increasing the offer price to JPY 2,270 and extending the acquisition period. This revision follows agreements with key shareholders, including the Hikari Tsushin Group, who will not tender their shares but will instead participate in a post-TOB share consolidation and a company share buyback at a different, tax-optimized price.

This revised structure aims to ensure a successful delisting by securing the necessary shareholder support for a squeeze-out, while offering a higher price to public shareholders and a tax-efficient exit for specific large shareholders.

  • Offer price increased from JPY 2,250 to JPY 2,270 per share.
  • Tender offer period extended from September 15, 2026, to October 5, 2026.
  • Minimum acquisition threshold reduced from 40.16% to 30.23% of outstanding shares.
  • Certain large shareholders will not tender but will participate in a post-TOB share buyback at a different price.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should note the improved offer price and extended period, alongside the complex post-TOB structure designed to facilitate delisting and accommodate specific large shareholders.

Tender Offer (TOB) ITG-G Holdings Co., Ltd.

ITG-G Holdings launches tender offer for Kadoya Sesame Mills Inc. at ¥2,514 per share.

ITG-G Holdings, a private equity-backed entity, has initiated a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Kadoya Sesame Mills Inc. with the aim of delisting the company. The offer price is ¥2,514 per share, with the potential for a higher price if major corporate shareholders opt for a share buyback. The target's board supports the offer, recommending shareholders to tender their shares.

This transaction reflects a growing trend in Japan where private equity-backed entities delist public companies to facilitate long-term strategic investments, particularly in new markets like the US, free from short-term market pressures. The structured process, involving an independent committee and consideration of multiple proposals, highlights evolving corporate governance standards.

  • Initial tender offer price: ¥2,514 per share.
  • Offer period: September 15, 2026, to October 30, 2026 (30 business days).
  • Minimum tender condition: 13,847,100 shares, representing 66.65% of voting rights.
  • Potential higher offer price: The offer price could increase to ¥2,644, ¥2,620, or ¥2,760 per share if major corporate shareholders (Mitsubishi Corp. and/or Mitsui & Co.) agree to a share buyback instead of tendering.
  • Delisting: Kadoya Sesame Mills Inc. will be delisted following the tender offer and subsequent squeeze-out procedures.
  • Funding: Up to ¥68,750 million to be provided indirectly by Integral Group funds.

Investor TakeawayShareholders should carefully evaluate the initial offer price of ¥2,514 and the potential for a higher price (up to ¥2,760) depending on the participation of major corporate shareholders in a share buyback, considering the board's recommendation and the certainty of delisting.

Tender Offer (TOB) Leopalace21 Corporation

Leopalace21 board supports tender offer for privatization at JPY 1,000 per share.

Leopalace21's board of directors has expressed support for a tender offer by K891 Corporation, a special purpose entity backed by Hikari Tsushin, MBKP Fund, and NECCS Fund, to acquire all outstanding shares and stock options. The company recommends shareholders tender their shares, citing the offer's fairness and the strategic benefits of privatization for long-term corporate value enhancement.

This move signifies Leopalace21's strategic shift to privatize, enabling it to pursue structural reforms and growth initiatives away from short-term market pressures, leveraging its new owners' capital and business synergies.

  • Tender offer price for common shares is JPY 1,000 per share.
  • Offer period is from September 15, 2026, to October 30, 2026 (30 business days).
  • Minimum tender quantity is 158,716,000 shares, representing 66.36% of voting rights, to facilitate subsequent squeeze-out procedures.
  • The offer price represents a 52.21% premium over the closing price on September 11, 2026.
  • The company's silver business will be divested in a separate transaction to Japan Innovation Investment.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should note the board's unanimous recommendation and the significant premium offered, as the company aims for full privatization, which will lead to delisting.

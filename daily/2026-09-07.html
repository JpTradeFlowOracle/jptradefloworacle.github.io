What is EDINET and why is this data important?

EDINET (Electronic Disclosure for Investors NETwork) is the official disclosure platform operated by Japan's Financial Services Agency. It contains mandatory filings including tender offers, large shareholder reports (5%+ ownership changes), extraordinary reports, and share buyback disclosures. M&A / Restructuring Grandys Co., Ltd.

Grandys consolidates Kanto subsidiaries to streamline operations and enhance group value.

Grandys announced an absorption-type merger between two wholly-owned subsidiaries, Morizo Co., Ltd. (surviving) and San-ai Home Co., Ltd. (dissolving).

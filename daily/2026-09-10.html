What is EDINET and why is this data important?

EDINET (Electronic Disclosure for Investors NETwork) is the official disclosure platform operated by Japan's Financial Services Agency. It contains mandatory filings including tender offers, large shareholder reports (5%+ ownership changes), extraordinary reports, and share buyback disclosures. JP Disclosure Intelligence structures and translates this data into English daily.

Tender Offer (TOB) MP-2606 Co., Ltd.

Private equity firm launches tender offer to acquire Sharing Technology for full ownership.

MP-2606 Co., Ltd., a special purpose entity of MBK Partners, has launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares and stock acquisition rights of Sharing Technology Co., Ltd. for JPY 1,550 per common share. The aim is to delist the target and implement strategic initiatives to enhance corporate value, including integrating with another MBK portfolio company, HITOWA, to address a challenging market environment and drive growth.

This transaction represents a strategic move by MBK Partners to consolidate and transform a platform business facing digital market shifts and labor shortages, leveraging private ownership for long-term value creation.

  • The offer price for common shares is JPY 1,550 per share.
  • The tender offer period runs from September 10, 2026, to October 27, 2026 (30 business days).
  • A minimum tender of 16,030,200 shares, representing 63.58% of voting rights, is required for the offer to succeed.
  • The target's largest shareholder, Asset Value Investors Limited (28.08% stake), has agreed to tender its shares.
  • The target's board supports the offer and recommends shareholders to tender their shares.
  • The JPY 39.37 billion acquisition is financed through loans from multiple major Japanese banks.

Investor TakeawayShareholders should note the offer price of JPY 1,550 per share, which exceeds all independent valuation ranges, and the target board's recommendation to tender, with a clear path to delisting if the minimum threshold is met.

Tender Offer (TOB) SharingTechnology Inc.

Company Board Recommends Tender Offer for Privatization at JPY 1,550.

SharingTechnology Inc.'s board has endorsed a tender offer by MP-2606, a subsidiary of MBK Partners funds, aiming to take the company private. The offer price is JPY 1,550 per common share, representing a significant premium over recent market prices. The privatization seeks to address challenges in digital marketing and labor, enabling strategic investments and M&A for long-term growth away from public market pressures.

This transaction marks a strategic shift for the company, moving to a private structure to facilitate agile decision-making and substantial investment, potentially enhancing its competitive position in the service platform sector.

  • Tender offer price for common shares is JPY 1,550.
  • Tender offer price for stock acquisition rights is JPY 1.
  • The offer period runs from September 10, 2026, to October 27, 2026.
  • The minimum tender condition is 16,030,200 shares, representing 63.58% of outstanding shares.
  • The offer price includes a 5.80% premium over the closing price on September 9, 2026.
  • The offer price includes a 55.78% premium over the closing price on June 26, 2026 (pre-speculation).
  • Major shareholder Asset Value Investors Limited (AVI) has agreed to tender its 7,078,700 shares (28.08% stake).

Investor TakeawayInvestors should consider the attractive premium offered and the board's recommendation in light of the impending delisting and the company's strategic rationale for going private.

Tender Offer (TOB) Kamgras 1 Corporation

Kamgras 1 raises tender offer price and extends period for target company.

Kamgras 1 has amended its tender offer for the target company, increasing the offer price per common share from ¥3,571 to ¥3,680 and extending the tender offer period until September 29, 2026. This adjustment aims to enhance the likelihood of a successful acquisition, particularly by addressing concerns from a key shareholder, Oasis, which had previously indicated it would not support an offer below ¥3,640.

The increased offer price and extended period reflect the bidder's commitment to securing the target company, potentially overcoming shareholder resistance and facilitating the privatization process.

  • The tender offer price for common shares increased from ¥3,571 to ¥3,680.
  • The tender offer period was extended from September 10, 2026, to September 29, 2026.
  • Total acquisition costs, including the purchase price and fees, increased from approximately ¥435.96 billion to ¥449.27 billion.
  • The capital contribution from the parent company increased from ¥211 billion to ¥225 billion.
  • The subsequent share buyback price for non-tendering shareholders was adjusted from ¥2,903 to ¥2,992.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should note the improved offer price and extended timeline, which signal the bidder's strong intent to complete the acquisition and may lead to a higher probability of success for the tender offer.

Kamgras 1 raises Kakaku.com tender offer price to JPY 3,680, extends period.

Kamgras 1 has revised its tender offer for Kakaku.com, increasing the per-share price and extending the offer period. This adjustment aims to enhance the offer's appeal to shareholders and increase the likelihood of a successful acquisition, particularly by addressing concerns from a significant institutional investor.

The increased offer price and extended timeline are critical for securing shareholder approval and completing the privatization, signaling a stronger commitment from the bidder to achieve the acquisition at a more favorable valuation for existing shareholders.

  • The tender offer price for common shares has been increased from JPY 3,571 to JPY 3,680 per share.
  • The tender offer period has been extended to September 29, 2026, resulting in a total of 95 business days.
  • The payment commencement date for tendered shares is now scheduled for October 6, 2026.
  • The revised price of JPY 3,680 surpasses the JPY 3,640 threshold previously indicated by major shareholder Oasis Management as a condition for their support.

Investor TakeawayInvestors should consider the improved offer as a potentially final and more attractive exit opportunity, reflecting the bidder's efforts to meet key shareholder expectations for a successful transaction.

This move secures the privatization of a key affiliate, potentially streamlining its strategic direction, and provides a substantial financial uplift for Digital Garage, impacting its upcoming fiscal year results.

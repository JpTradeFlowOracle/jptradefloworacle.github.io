Tender Offer (TOB) 15:00
Sakura Rubber Co., Ltd. 桜ゴム

Sakura Rubber completes divestiture, recognizing substantial extraordinary gain.

Sakura Rubber has successfully tendered its entire stake in Japan Dry Chemical through a tender offer, resulting in a significant extraordinary gain. This strategic divestment monetizes a non-core asset, enhancing the company's financial position.

The transaction demonstrates a move to optimize the investment portfolio and improve capital efficiency, providing a substantial one-time boost to earnings that could support future strategic initiatives or shareholder returns.

  • Divested 192,000 shares of Japan Dry Chemical.
  • Total proceeds from the sale reached ¥716 million.
  • Recognized an extraordinary gain of ¥683 million from the sale of investment securities.
  • The transaction settled on July 6, 2026.
  • The gain will be recorded in the first quarter of the fiscal year ending March 2027.
Investor Takeaway Investors should note the significant one-time gain, which has already been incorporated into the company's recently revised full-year earnings and dividend forecasts.

Kaga Electronics extends Shinko Shoji tender offer period, maintains original price.

Kaga Electronics is proceeding with its tender offer for Shinko Shoji, aiming for full acquisition. This development highlights a strategic move by Kaga Electronics to consolidate its position in the electronics sector, despite resistance on valuation from the target. The extended timeline provides a longer window for shareholders to consider the offer.

  • The tender offer price remains 1,580 yen per share.
  • The tender offer period is extended until August 3, 2026.
  • Kaga Electronics aims to acquire all common shares of Shinko Shoji.
  • Shinko Shoji's board recommends shareholders tender their shares.
Investor Takeaway Investors should note the extended timeline for the tender offer and Kaga Electronics' firm stance on the offer price, indicating a long-term commitment to the acquisition.

This move solidifies CAICA DIGITAL's strategic influence over FISCO and signals a clear pivot in their joint business alliance towards leveraging AI and DX capabilities in financial services, aligning with broader industry trends.

  • CAICA DIGITAL acquired 2,937,500 FISCO shares from JN Group.
  • CAICA DIGITAL's voting rights in FISCO increased to 24.13% (from 17.72%).
  • JN Group's voting rights in FISCO decreased to 23.70% (from 30.12%).
  • FISCO holds 11.74% of CAICA DIGITAL's voting rights.
  • The updated capital and business alliance agreement was signed on July 29, 2026.
Investor Takeaway Investors should assess the governance implications of CAICA DIGITAL's increased control and the potential for enhanced value creation from the refocused AI and DX alliance.

Tender Offer (TOB) 15:30
Metalart Co., Ltd. メタルアート

Tender offer for Metalart shares by Gerbera holdings fails to meet minimum threshold.

Gerbera holdings Inc.'s tender offer for Metalart Co., Ltd. shares concluded without success. The offer, which ran from May 15 to July 28, 2026, failed to attract the minimum required number of shares, resulting in no shares being acquired.

This outcome means the proposed change in ownership structure will not proceed, maintaining the current shareholder composition and potentially impacting future strategic directions for Metalart.

  • The tender offer period was from May 15, 2026, to July 28, 2026.
  • The offer price was JPY 7,713 per common share.
  • Only 721,295 shares were tendered, falling short of the 849,600 share minimum threshold.
  • No shares will be purchased by Gerbera holdings Inc.
Investor Takeaway Investors should note the failure of the tender offer means no immediate change in control or significant shareholder structure for Metalart.

Earnings Forecast 09:00
Tokuyama Corporation トクヤマ

Tokuyama Corporation announces initial FY2027 earnings and increased dividend forecasts.

Tokuyama Corporation has released its full-year consolidated earnings forecast for fiscal year 2027, which was previously undecided, alongside an updated dividend forecast. The company projects significant revenue and net income growth compared to the prior fiscal year, providing clarity on its financial outlook. This forecast excludes the impact of certain pending transactions related to cement sales and subsidiary share transfers, which are still under evaluation.

This announcement provides critical financial guidance after a period of uncertainty, potentially stabilizing investor expectations. The projected growth and increased dividend signal management's confidence in the company's operational performance and commitment to enhanced shareholder returns, aligning with its long-term dividend policy.

  • For FY2027, the company forecasts consolidated revenue of ¥392,000 million, operating profit of ¥34,000 million, and net income attributable to owners of parent of ¥26,000 million.
  • The projected FY2027 earnings per share is ¥361.38.
  • The annual dividend forecast for FY2027 is ¥132.00 per share (interim ¥60.00, year-end ¥72.00), an increase from FY2026's ¥120.00.
  • The company's dividend policy targets a DOE (Dividend on Equity) of 4.0% by FY2030, with the FY2027 forecast representing a DOE of 3.4%.
  • For reference, FY2026 actuals were revenue of ¥349,476 million, operating profit of ¥37,071 million, and net income of ¥22,207 million.
Investor Takeaway Investors should note the positive outlook for revenue and net income growth, coupled with a higher dividend, indicating a stable financial trajectory and commitment to shareholder returns.

Earnings Forecast 15:00
Sakura Rubber Co., Ltd. 桜ゴム

Sakura Rubber establishes FY2027 forecast, projecting higher net profit and dividends despite operating profit decline.

Sakura Rubber has released its first full-year consolidated earnings forecast for FY2027, previously undisclosed due to supply chain uncertainties. The company anticipates a significant increase in net profit attributable to the parent due to gains from policy-held share sales, alongside a higher annual dividend. However, operating and ordinary profits are projected to decline year-over-year due to persistent high raw material costs.

The establishment of a clear forecast provides much-needed clarity for investors amidst geopolitical risks, while the strategic divestment of policy-held shares boosts net income and shareholder returns, aligning with governance reforms.

  • FY2027 net profit attributable to parent is forecast at 800 million JPY, up from 666 million JPY in FY2026.
  • FY2027 operating profit is forecast at 690 million JPY, a decrease from 1,231 million JPY in FY2026.
  • The company announced a total annual dividend of 120 JPY per share for FY2027, an increase from 100 JPY in FY2026.
  • The revised dividend forecast implies a consolidated payout ratio of approximately 29%.
Investor Takeaway Investors should note the company's improved net profit and dividend payout driven by non-operating gains and a commitment to shareholder returns, even as core operational profitability faces headwinds from input costs.
